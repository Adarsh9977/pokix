Two autonomous AI agents enter a tactical arena. They observe the same evolving world, make simultaneous typed decisions through TypeSafe's Jev model, and a deterministic engine turns those decisions into consequences.

Jev decides what an agent wants to do.
The game engine decides what actually happens.
